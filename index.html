7 Days is an open-world game that is a unique combination of first-person shooter, survival horror, tower defense, and role-playing games. It presents combat, crafting, looting, mining, exploration, and character growth. The story unfolds after a devastating nuclear war caused by the Septdiurnal Virus (SDV), which transforms the remaining population into aggressive zombies.

The 7 Days to Die map shows your character's location, time, temperature, and elevation, along with biomes (Forest, Burnt Forest, Desert, Wasteland, Snow) and water sources after exploration. Each biome has different challenges, resources, and loot. Specific map markers, such as brown squares for Iron Ore, small black squares for Coal Ore, and small blue squares for Lead Ore, reveal resource deposits.

Horde Nights (also known as Blood Moons) are periodic, intense events that occur every seven in-game days, beginning at 10 PM and ending at 4 AM. During a Horde Night, a large number of aggressive zombies, who know the player's location, relentlessly attack the player's base.

The time cycle in 7 Days to Die is fully customizable, allowing players to adjust both the real-world duration of a 24-hour day and the length of the in-game daylight hours. These settings directly impact gameplay, including the behavior of zombies and the amount of time you have to prepare for the weekly "Blood Moon" horde.

The zombie businessman in 7 Days to Die is a standard zombie type that can become extremely dangerous in its advanced forms. While they are not a special infected with unique attacks, their powerful, high-tier variants can do significantly more damage than other zombie types.

Radiated is a condition that affects Zombies though not all zombies have a Radiated variant. Radiated is currently the highest level variant above Feral and below the unreleased Legendary variant. This condition will leave the Zombie with very high Health, and less sensitive to pain leaving Radiated zombies less likely to stagger when attacked. Radiated Zombies also have the ability to slowly regenerate health over time, which can be temporarily disabled with the Rad Remover mod.

Bear are wild animals commonly found in the Forest, and Snowy Forest biomes. Bears are hostile and will try to chase and attack the player if provoked or if they spot the player.

Zombie Dogs are zombie animals found in every biome but more commonly in the Wasteland. Zombie Dogs are hostile and will try to chase and attack the player if provoked or spots the player. Zombie Dogs can at times be heard growling letting players know they are discovered and about to be attacked.

Mountain Lions are dangerous animals found in the Snowy Forest biome. They can also roam within Snowy Forest towns.

Guns are categorized into four types: Handguns/SMGs, Shotguns, Rifles, and Machine Guns. Each category has multiple tiers, from early-game pipe weapons to high-end military-grade firearms.

The truck is the 4x4 Truck, a high-end ground vehicle prized for its immense storage capacity and speed.
